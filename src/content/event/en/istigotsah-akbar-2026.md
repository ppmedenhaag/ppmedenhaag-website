Bismillaahirrahmaanirrahiim.

Assalaamu 'alaykum wa rochmatullahi wa barokaatuh 🙏

To all members and board members of PPME.

Seeking the pleasure of Allah SWT, we cordially invite you to the **Istighotsah Akbar PPME**, which will take place, InshaAllah, on:

📅 **Date:** 18 Djoemad il Aakhir 1448 (Saturday 28 November 2026)<br/>
⏰ **Time:** 12:00 - 19:00 CET<br/>
📍 **Location:** Masdjid Al-Hikmah, Meddlerstraat 4, The Hague

#### Programme

- Opening
- Updates from the PPME branches in the Netherlands
- Kalam Asaatiedz (words from the ustadz)
- Istiegótsah & Dziekr
- Closing
- Dinner

Let us come together, strengthen our brotherhood (ukhuwah), and pray for unity and blessings for all PPME branches.

Jazakumullahu khairan katsiran for your attention and attendance.

Languages: **Indonesian and Dutch**.

#### Registration

Dear attendees, thank you to everyone who has already registered for the upcoming Istigotsah Akbar. If you have not registered yet, please do so as soon as possible so that we can prepare the food properly. InshaAllah.
